## Main Figure Caption

Figure X. MAGI fingerprint profiles for three manuscript case studies. (A) MAT1A p.Gly336Arg (NM_000429.3:c.1006G>A; p.Gly336Arg) shows a dominant exon shift (-0.5664) and strongest contextual change in breast carcinoma cell line | CAGE (+0.1797). (B) ALOX15B rs9895916 (NM_001141.3:c.1457G>A; p.Arg486His) shows a dominant intron shift (+0.4238) and strongest contextual change in blood vessel | RNA-seq [1] (-0.3477). (C) COL4A2 chr13:110492070 G>A (NM_001846.4:c.3455G>A; p.Gly1152Asp) shows a dominant exon shift (-0.3555) and strongest contextual change in brain | RNA-seq [1] (-0.4102). Bars report Alt minus Ref delta probabilities for the top 15 disrupted MAGI signals. Source panels were exported at 600 DPI with matched PDF and SVG files; this assembled figure is rendered at 400 DPI for manuscript layout.

## Supplementary Figure Caption

Supplementary Figure X. Local region-track views for the manuscript case studies. (A) MAT1A p.Gly336Arg highlights local REF versus ALT profile changes centered on the variant, with the strongest track-level effects in exon (-0.5664), ORF (-0.5371), intron (+0.4570). (B) ALOX15B rs9895916 highlights local REF versus ALT profile changes centered on the variant, with the strongest track-level effects in intron (+0.4238), exon (-0.4082), ORF (-0.3984). (C) COL4A2 chr13:110492070 G>A highlights local REF versus ALT profile changes centered on the variant, with the strongest track-level effects in brain | RNA-seq [1] (-0.4102), liver | RNA-seq [1] (-0.3945), brain | RNA-seq [2] (-0.3828). Dashed vertical lines mark the variant position; red shading denotes gain and blue shading denotes loss relative to the reference prediction.
