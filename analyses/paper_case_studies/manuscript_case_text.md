# Manuscript Results Text

## Cross-Case Paragraph

Across the three case studies, MAGI recovered coherent but distinct local disruption signatures. MAT1A NM_000429.3:c.1006G>A (p.Gly336Arg) produced the strongest exon-centered loss signal in the set (exon -0.5664, top absolute delta +0.5664, LLR -1.782), consistent with a coding variant that also perturbs exon-definition features. ALOX15B NM_001141.3:c.1457G>A (p.Arg486His) showed a reciprocal intron gain and exon/ORF loss pattern (intron +0.4238, top contextual change in blood vessel | RNA-seq [1] -0.3477), but with weaker sequence-model support (LLR -0.295), arguing for a more moderate mechanistic effect. COL4A2 NM_001846.4:c.3455G>A (p.Gly1152Asp) combined strong coding-track disruption with broad transcriptomic losses, yielding the most negative sequence-model score among the three cases (LLR -1.849).

## COL4A2 Paragraph

The newly added COL4A2 variant, NM_001846.4:c.3455G>A (p.Gly1152Asp), maps to a coding exon in the MANE Select transcript rather than to a canonical splice-donor position. MAGI assigns a strong loss of exon probability (-0.3555) together with a reciprocal intron gain (+0.3203 in the top-track table) and substantial losses across brain and liver RNA-seq contexts, with the strongest contextual effect in brain | RNA-seq [1] (-0.4102). The aggregate scores remain high (BED impact 0.317, BigWig impact 0.318, MAGI score 2650.06), and the negative LLR (-1.849) indicates that the alternate sequence is disfavored by the sequence model. Taken together, the pattern supports a missense-associated coding disruption with a pronounced local exon-structure signature, and the manuscript text should describe it as a coding COL4A2 case rather than as a splice-donor example.
